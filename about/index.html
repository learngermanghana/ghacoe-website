About GHACOE

Rooted in faith.
Growing together.

We are the Ghana Congress on Evangelisation Women’s Ministry, a non-denominational Christian ministry in Ghana.

Our purpose

Faith in action

Our work connects spiritual growth with practical opportunities for women, young people and communities. We bring people together through fellowship, skills training and outreach.

We aim to create space to learn, serve, build confidence and support one another.
