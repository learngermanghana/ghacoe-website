We are the Ghana Congress on Evangelisation Women’s Ministry: a non-denominational Christian organization in Ghana.

Our ministry brings spiritual growth together with the holistic and economic development of women, young people and vulnerable groups. Through fellowship, practical training and outreach, we create space for people to learn, serve and grow.

Talk to the ministry →
Is GHACOE part of one denomination?

GHACOE Women’s Ministry is a non-denominational Christian organization in Ghana. Its work brings together spiritual growth, practical development and community care.

How can I volunteer?

Choose “Volunteer With Us” to view the application and share your skills and availability when applications open. The ministry reviews requests and follows up about suitable opportunities.

What skills training is available?

The Ideal Womanhood Project includes skills such as soap making, batik and tie-dye, bead making, catering, fashion and cosmetology. Ask the ministry which programmes are currently accepting enquiries. Dates, fees and places are confirmed by the ministry.

Where can I find conference dates?

Confirmed dates and registration details will appear in Gatherings. For now, follow the ministry’s published social updates. No unconfirmed event dates are listed here.

Can I make a donation online?

Online giving will open after the ministry’s payment connection is ready. When enabled, checkout continues securely through Paystack. The website does not collect your card details.

How do I ask for support?

Use “Request support” to view the form. When applications open, describe the kind of help you need and provide a contact method. Requests are reviewed by the ministry; submitting does not guarantee assistance. Please do not include sensitive documents.
