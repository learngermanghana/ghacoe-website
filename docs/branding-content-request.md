# GHACOE Women’s Ministry — content and branding request

**Status:** Pre-launch. The red, blue and white palette in this branch is a *proposed interpretation* of the ministry's colours. Request approval of exact official shades before publishing.

## 1. Official identity
- Approved GHACOE Women’s Ministry logo (transparent PNG and preferably SVG) plus any ministry or umbrella-organisation logo that must also appear.
- Official red, blue and white colour codes or an existing letterhead/brand guide.
- Correct full ministry name, tagline, approved spelling and attribution.

## 2. Photographs (priority)
Please send original, high-resolution photos rather than screenshots, ideally with a brief caption identifying the activity:
- **Homepage:** 1 strong landscape or portrait of a real GHACOE gathering or activity to replace the current *illustrative stock image*.
- **Faith & Fellowship:** 2–3 photos of ministry prayer, conferences, fellowship or group meetings.
- **Skills & Opportunity:** 2–3 photos of approved workshops or skills training in action.
- **Care & Outreach:** 2–3 photos showing real ministry outreach or community projects.
- **Leadership:** Optional official individual/group portraits with names and titles.

Only send photos GHACOE has permission to publish. Check that everyone pictured has appropriate consent, especially children and people receiving assistance. Send photo credits or photographer requirements where relevant. Do not send sensitive beneficiary information.

## 3. Approved text and factual details
- Short ministry history, mission, vision and key focus areas.
- Leadership names and roles, and a short welcome message if desired.
- Current/confirmed programmes and activities, including whom they serve and how to join.
- Upcoming events: title, confirmed dates, venues, registration links and flyers.
- Official address/location, phone, public email and social-media links.
- Verified volunteer, partnership, support and donation policies, plus any privacy wording required for public forms.

## 4. Account and publishing
- Confirm which platform the new administrator account was created for and the intended access role.
- **Do not send passwords, one-time codes, private API keys or payment credentials in chat or by email.**
- Confirm who will approve website text, images and the final design before launch.

## Implementation notes
- Keep the website's pre-launch notices until information and integrations have been confirmed.
- Replace illustrative images only with approved ministry photos.
- No changes to payment/forms integration or published public claims without separate confirmation.
